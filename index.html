01 / About

Engineered for the Kingdom

Founded in 1973 by Mr. Naif Obaid Al Shammari and Mr. Jufal Obaid Al Shammari, JNCO is an industrial and commercial engineering firm with diversified experience across civil, mechanical and electrical construction.

From our headquarters in Ras Tanura, we have delivered infrastructure for the Kingdom's most demanding clients — Saudi Aramco, SABIC, the Royal Commission for Jubail & Yanbu, the Ministry of Defense and Aviation, and many others.

Our Mission

To deliver quality work performance strictly in accordance with local and international standards, ensuring the highest professionalism and client satisfaction in every undertaking.

Our Vision
